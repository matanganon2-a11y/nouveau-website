Questions?

Call the location nearest you and our team will help.

Does it hurt? Is there downtime?

No needles, no surgery and no recovery time. Most clients describe the treatments as relaxing. Skin can look slightly flushed for a short while after cryo or LED.

What do I get for $79.99?

A skin consultation with a licensed esthetician plus one face or body treatment. It is for new clients, once per person, at any of our five locations.

How soon will I see results?

Many clients see a brighter, fresher look after one facial. Body contouring and firming work best as a series, and your esthetician will map that out with you.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No. Book online and our team will call to confirm your appointment.

Can I pay over time?

Yes. CareCredit financing is available at every location, with plans from 3 to 24 months.

Do you sell gift cards?

Yes. Gift cards work for any treatment at any Nouveau location. Ask at the front desk or call your nearest center.
