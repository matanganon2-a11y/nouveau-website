Florida sun and your skin: a year-round routine that works

Nouveau Wellness Center5 min readUpdated October 2026
The short answer

In Florida, SPF is a year-round habit, not a summer one. Keep the routine simple: gentle cleanse, hydration, daily broad-spectrum SPF and regular professional facials to keep skin clear and hydrated.

In Southwest and Central Florida, sunny days do not stop in winter. Heat and humidity also change how skin behaves. A routine that works here looks a little different from one built for colder places.

The four-step daily routine

  1. Gentle cleanser. Heat and humidity mean more sweat and oil. Cleanse morning and night without stripping your skin.
  2. Lightweight hydration. A light gel or lotion moisturizer is often enough in humid months. Heavier creams can feel better when air conditioning dries the air indoors.
  3. Broad-spectrum SPF every morning. Every day, including cloudy days and days you mostly drive. UVA light passes through car and window glass.
  4. Reapply when you are outside. Beach, pool, golf or a long walk: reapply about every two hours and after swimming or sweating.

Where professional facials fit

  • HydroFacial: clears the buildup that heat, sweat and sunscreen can leave in pores.
  • Cryo Facial + LED: calms skin that looks red or tired after time outdoors.
  • Nano Facial: focused hydration for skin that feels dry from sun and air conditioning.

Many clients book one facial a month as a reset, and adjust the treatment with the seasons.

Small habits that add up

  • A wide-brimmed hat and sunglasses for midday sun.
  • Drinking water through the day, especially when it is hot.
  • Changing pillowcases often, especially if you get breakouts.
