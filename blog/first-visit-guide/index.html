Your first visit to Nouveau: exactly what happens

Nouveau Wellness Center5 min readUpdated October 2026
The short answer

You pick a time online, we call to confirm, your esthetician looks at your skin and talks through your goals, you have your treatment, and you leave with a clear plan. No deposit, no needles, no pressure.

Trying a new place for your skin can feel like a lot. Here is the whole visit, step by step, so nothing comes as a surprise.

Before you arrive

  1. You book onlineChoose your location and a time that works. It takes about a minute.
  2. We call youOur team calls shortly after to confirm the time and answer questions. If you have a skin concern or a date you are preparing for, mention it here.
  3. You get a reminderA text reminder arrives before your appointment, with the address.

How to prepare

  • Come with clean skin if you can, or with light makeup. We cleanse before every treatment either way.
  • Skip exfoliating scrubs and strong acids for a couple of days before a facial.
  • Let us know about any medication that affects your skin, recent procedures or if you are pregnant.
  • Plan to arrive about 10 minutes early for a short intake form.

At your visit

  1. Skin consultationYour esthetician looks at your skin, asks about your routine and what you want to change, and explains which treatment fits.
  2. Your $79.99 treatmentA face or body treatment, usually 30 to 60 minutes. Most clients describe it as relaxing.
  3. Your planBefore you leave, you get a written recommendation: what to do at home and, if you want it, which treatments would build on today's result.

Will I be pressured to buy a package?

No. Your esthetician will explain the options so you can decide, but there is no obligation. Many clients come back for a single visit first and decide later.

After your visit

  • You can go straight back to work or your day.
  • Use a gentle cleanser and SPF for the next 24 hours.
  • If skin looks slightly flushed after cryo or LED, that usually settles quickly.
This article is general information about cosmetic, non-invasive treatments and everyday skincare. It is not medical advice. Results vary from person to person. If you have a skin condition, are pregnant, or take medication that affects your skin, talk to your doctor before starting any treatment.
