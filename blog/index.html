Skincare 101

5 signs your skin needs a HydroFacial

Dryness, dullness and clogged pores are common, so how do you know it's time for a professional HydroFacial instead of more moisturizer? Watch for flakiness that stays even after hydrating, a dull or uneven tone, rough texture, recurring breakouts around the nose and chin, and makeup that won't sit smoothly. A HydroFacial cleanses, exfoliates and hydrates in one session, which is why it's one of our most requested treatments before events and at the change of seasons.

Body contouring

CryoSkin vs. Lipo Laser: which is right for you?

Both are non-surgical ways to work on stubborn areas, and they work differently. CryoSkin uses controlled cooling over a series of sessions, a gentle option for toning areas like the abdomen or thighs. Lipo Laser with ultrasonic cavitation combines low-level laser and ultrasound for contouring. The right choice depends on your goals, the area and your timeline, and our team will help you decide at your first visit.

Skincare 101

Why 14K Gold Facials are more than a trend

Gold facials are known for luxury, but the appeal goes beyond the glow. Gold-infused serums are paired with massage techniques that support circulation and help reduce the look of puffiness. Combined with antioxidant-rich serums, skin looks brighter and more radiant right after a single session, which makes it a favorite before special occasions.

Treatment guide

How LED light therapy fits a long-term routine

Different LED wavelengths are used for different goals. Red light is used for a smoother, more even-looking complexion, while blue light is used in routines for breakout-prone skin. Paired with cryo in our signature face and neck lift, LED works best as part of a regular maintenance routine rather than a one-time fix.

Wellness

Building a self-care routine that actually sticks

The biggest barrier to a consistent routine is overcomplication. Start with one anchor habit, like a monthly facial, and build from there. Pair professional treatments with simple daily habits such as SPF, sleep and hydration. Our team can help you plan a realistic treatment rhythm for your goals and budget at your first visit.
