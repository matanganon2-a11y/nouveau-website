HydroFacial vs. Nano Facial: which one should you book?

Book a HydroFacial if your main goal is clean, clear, hydrated skin. Book a Nano Facial if your skin is already fairly clear and you want serums focused on hydration, tone or firmness. Many clients alternate between the two.

Body contouring

CryoSkin vs. Lipo Laser: how to choose a body treatment

Both are non-surgical and need no recovery time. CryoSkin uses controlled cooling and is a gentle way to tone specific areas. Lipo Laser pairs low-level laser with ultrasound for contouring. Your goal, the area and your timeline decide which fits.

The technology

Red vs. blue LED light: what each color is used for

Red light is used in routines for a smoother, more even-looking complexion. Blue light is used in routines for breakout-prone skin. Sessions are painless, need no recovery and work best as part of a regular routine.
